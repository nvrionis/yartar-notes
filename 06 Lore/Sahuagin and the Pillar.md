# Sahuagin and the Pillar

At [[Noannar's Hold]], a sahuagin caster used the **[[Blue Gem]]** to raise a pillar of seawater inside a sealed temple, out of nothing. Sahuagin came out of the pillar. The water did not stop rising afterwards, and the town went under.

[[The Kraken Society]] was behind it, by [[The KrakenWizard]]'s own dying word.

> [!question] The gem made an ocean where there was none. That is not a raid — that is terraforming. Who wanted the Hold underwater, and are there other gems?
