# Time as Memory
*[[The Professor]]'s thesis*

That time is not a substance but **a collection of memories** — and that the [[Vanishing Tower]] of [[Ilvaeris]] is the proof, if someone will go in and fetch it.

Expeditions through the rift are the experiment. [[Memory Shards]] are the data.

It is the fourth time the campaign has put the same idea in front of the party. See [[Perception and Reality]].

> [!cael] Cael
> If the past is made of memory rather than stone, then it is made of what people hold — and what people hold can be given to them. Cael has believed something adjacent to this his whole life without ever hearing it stated as a theory. He is not going to say so out loud.

> [!question] He is simultaneously trying to **close** the rifts and to keep **using** them. Which of those is the real project?
