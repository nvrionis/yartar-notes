# Eldath and the Older Temple

The temple at [[Noannar's Hold]] was a house of **Eldath**, goddess of peace — built on a site that had belonged to **Leira**, the Lady of Mists, in ages past.

[[Sol]] the sailor knew the site was older. [[Cael]] confirmed it.

The people of the Hold prayed there in good faith and had no idea. Whether it mattered to their prayers, nobody can say — and then the temple became a slaughterhouse and the whole town drowned, which does not settle the question either way.
