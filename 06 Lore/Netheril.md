# Netheril
The ancient ruins in which [[The Orc Horde]] is camped, outside [[The Professor's Tower]], are **Netherese** — identified after the fact.

A wizard studying rifts in time has built his tower in a Netherese ruin. That is unlikely to be a coincidence.
