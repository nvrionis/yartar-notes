# Perception and Reality

A running thread, gathered here because it keeps coming back.

- A town praying to the wrong goddess and none the worse for it — [[Eldath and the Older Temple]].
- [[Vaelis]] taking the last breath of the dying and borrowing how they saw the world at the end.
- [[Cael]] talking [[Devon Dale]] into surviving by telling her to **shift her perception** — the city, not the group.
- [[The Professor]] holding that time itself is [[Time as Memory|made of memory]].
- [[Cael]]'s own trade: a blessing is a way of getting a man to believe the moment is his, and belief moves the outcome.
