# Previously On

*Read aloud at the start of play. Regenerated after every session by `/recap`.*

---

Three of you are dying, slowly, and none of you know what from.

The poison went in at **the Last Pint**, the morning after you played that room as **the Harpies Lyras**. **The Knife** sold you a half-cure for the direction **Devon Dale** ran — the woman you were hired to save, the woman **Cael** wore out of Duskandale so she could run invisible under her own face. She never reached her extraction. Nobody knows where she is, and the people who took her documents are gone too.

The only real antidote is east. East is a drawbridge that hasn't moved in an age. East is ten people **Cael** pulled off a road the orcs had already finished with, who will tell that story for the rest of their lives. And east, finally, is a tower ringed by sixty of them, camped in ruins older than the kingdom that named them.

You went in invisible. **The Professor** — Lorien's old teacher, and not a steady man — is brewing your cure right now, alone, while you are somewhere else entirely.

Because the price was an errand. Through the rift, into the **Vanishing Tower** of Ilvaeris, god of time and memory and history.

There are columns here made of memory. There are things living in them that feed on it.

You have a rift to close, shards to carry home, and no way out but the one you came in by.
