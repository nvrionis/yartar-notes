# Epic Moments

The beats worth remembering out loud. Keep this short and keep it earned.

---

### The cavern under the corpses
`Session 0.5` · [[Noannar's Hold]]
[[Cael]] and [[Simon]] lived because a small hollow opened under a mountain of the dead. Nothing they did earned it.
> [!cael] Cael
> Luck, he says, is the moment before a thing has settled. He has never been closer to the moment, and he has never been less sure who settled it.

### The child in the paladin's arms
`Session 0.5`
[[Brian]] died in [[James D Folly]]'s hands after the water came down into the caves. The paladin has not been the same.

### Devon's performance
`Session 1` · [[The Hearthstone]], [[Duskandale]]
[[Devon Dale]] refused to run and told the room the truth instead — the [[The Ashrit Circle|Circle]], the [[The Hand of Yartar|Hand]], all of it. A magnificent piece of storytelling.
> [!cael] Cael
> He judged it honest, and decided on the spot she was worth saving. That judgment is his one real vanity. It has been wrong before, expensively, and he chose to trust it anyway. **Character beat — flag for the DM.**

### Wearing her face out the door
`Session 1`
[[Cael]] took [[Devon Dale]]'s place and sent her running invisible, steering her eyes away from [[Tak]]'s body on the way out. The village never knew. Only [[The Knife]] saw through it — because [[Vaelis]] told him.

### Ten off the road
`Session 3` · east of [[The Drawbridge]]
Medicine, prayer and magic, spent to the last drop, and nearly ten people who were going to die did not. [[Torvin]]'s daughter among them.
> [!cael] Cael
> To an uprooted village on a bad road, he is now a story with a name in it. He notices that he did not hate this.
