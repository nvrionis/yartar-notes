# Corrections and additions

Dump anything here, in any form, whenever you notice it. Half sentences are fine. This is not a note anyone reads for pleasure — it is an inbox, and `/session` empties it.

Use it for three things:

- **Wrong.** The vault says something that is not true, or says more than it should.
- **Missing.** Something real that never made it into the bullets — a familiar, an item, a name, who actually carries what.
- **Overstated.** The vault has turned a throwaway into a fact. The Harpies Lyras was one of these.

Delete each line once it has been folded in.
