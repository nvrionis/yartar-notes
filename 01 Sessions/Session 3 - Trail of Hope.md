# Session 3 — Trail of Hope

## Back for the horses

The party set out for the tower and the mage east that [[Vetel Kraye]] had named, and went back toward [[Duskandale]] first — it was close, and their horses were there.

They kept off the road and away from people. They passed a group gathered together, and **[[Cael]] saw [[Tak]] among them, alive, talking to them.**

Nobody has an explanation. See [[Open Threads]].

Then east, along the southern edge of the [[Evermoors]].

## The drawbridge

[[The Drawbridge]] — an old, magnificent piece of engineering, long dead. With [[Lorien]]'s magic they brought it back to working order and got it into position.

> [!cael] Cael
> While he was on the ropes, inside Lorien's magic, time slipped for him. Briefly, and wrongly. He has told nobody.

## Ten off the road

On the far side: the aftermath of an orc attack on what looked like a caravan and was actually **a whole village of the [[Evermoors]]**, uprooted and moving for better ground.

[[Cael]] spent everything he had — medicine, prayer, magic — and pulled close to ten people back from certain death. To those people he is now a story with his name in it. [[Lorien]] gave them the party's horses and 3 pp.

Their guide, **[[Dell]]**, promised the caravan would be steadfast allies if they met again. Lorien sent them toward [[Duskandale]], and suggested they might meet there in a few days — if the party survived.

**[[Torvin]]**, a half-elf whose daughter Cael had saved, gave him an old family heirloom: [[Torvin's Shield]].

## The horde

Moving east, hidden at the roadside with the help of Cael and the mists, they came in sight of [[The Professor's Tower]] — surrounded by the same [[The Orc Horde|orc horde]] that had hit the caravan.

Fifty to sixty of them, not all fighters. A towering female orc leads them. They are camped in ancient ruins, later learned to be **[[Netheril|Netherese]]**.

## Inside

The party made contact with the tower and were given assurances of safe passage if they could reach the door. They went in invisible, and the door opened.

**[[Beat]]** met them: a construct in the service of the tower's master wizard, with a worldview entirely his own. They talked him into fetching antitoxins from the wizard's locked laboratory, against the wizard's standing instruction not to be disturbed.

Here they also learned the [[The Ravensworth|Ravensworth]] siblings' names: **[[Doman Vein]]** and **[[Iskra Vein]]**.

## The Professor

The master of the tower — [[The Professor]] for now — is working to close rifts between the material realm and the **[[Vanishing Tower]]** of **[[Ilvaeris]]**, god of time, memories and history.

He holds that time is, at bottom, a collection of memories. The trips into the Vanishing Tower are an experiment to prove it. He does not look stable.

He was [[Lorien]]'s mentor.

## The terms

He will prepare the antidote. In exchange, the party goes through the rift in his tower into the Vanishing Tower, closes the rift on the far side, and brings back as many [[Memory Shards]] as they can.

They accepted.

## Through

The inside is a realm of its own. Columns made of memory. Things living in it that feed on memory. Everything alien, everything dangerous.
