# Session 0.5 — Noannar's Hold Massacre

## Before

[[Cael]] was given the [[Coin of Tymora]] by an old mentor from his road years — holed through the middle, and smooth on the tails side where something used to be. It is magical. What it does is not established.

## The Hold

Cael was travelling as bodyguard to an elf clerk, part of a caravan that stopped at [[Noannar's Hold]].

He heard from [[Sol]] — an old sailor missing an eye, a leg and an arm, all on his right side — that the temple of Eldath in the Hold stood on something older. He went to look. He found that the site had belonged to **Leira** in ages past, and that a town's worth of people now prayed to Eldath on top of her.

> [!cael] Cael
> He found this funnier than he let on, and said nothing to anyone.

## The massacre

A gathering was called at the temple. It was an ambush.

The doors were barricaded from outside. A pillar of seawater rose inside the temple out of nothing, and sahuagin came out of it. What followed was not a fight. Almost nobody got out.

[[Cael]] and [[Simon]] lived by falling into a small hollow beneath the piled dead. [[Silvio]], a gnome, survived long enough to press his amulet on them and ask that it reach his wife and three children in **Silverymoon**. He died in their hands.

## The tunnels

After a long rest they followed the hollow down into a cave system, and found others already in it: [[Vaelis]], [[James D Folly]], [[Wong Bless]], and a child, [[Brian]], attached to James.

That group had been sent to retrieve a **[[Blue Gem]]** from the temple. Together they came back up through the aftermath.

## The gem

A hard fight with sahuagin. One was a caster, and it was he who had used the [[Blue Gem]] to raise the pillar that destroyed the Hold. There was also a wizard, whom [[Vaelis]] destroyed — [[The KrakenWizard]], who gave his name and, dying, promised that the party would regret interfering with **[[The Kraken Society]]**.

The water kept coming. [[Noannar's Hold]] went under.

[[Brian]] died in [[James D Folly]]'s arms in the aftermath, when the water came down into the caves. It broke something in the paladin.

## After

The party split. [[Cael]] stayed with [[Vaelis]] and they talked about where they had come from.

Cael learned that Vaelis gathers the last breath of those he kills, and that this eases their passing — that he sees, briefly, how the dying perceive the world at the end. Cael found this fascinating rather than grim. He also came away certain that Vaelis is close to death himself, and detached in a way that is not ordinary.

They work well together regardless.

> [!cael] Cael
> A whole town praying at the wrong altar and never once the worse for it. A dying man's perception, borrowed by his killer. Cael did not survive that temple with a lesson about violence. He came out of it more convinced than ever that what people believe is doing load-bearing work in the world.
