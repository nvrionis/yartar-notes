# Session 1 — The Savage Daughter

## Yartar, briefly

Groundwork laid this session — full note at [[Yartar]].

[[Caldren Voss]] holds the city, taken by coup. He executed and burned his predecessor; **her handprint is still visible on the side of the bridge.** He styles himself a baron of the people, for the people. Many disagree.

[[The Hand of Yartar]] is gaining ground: daggers, poison, information. Membership is women only. They hire male goons.

Districts: [[Merchant Quarter]], [[Waterfront]], [[The Warrens]], [[High Ward]], [[Temple District]].

## The job

A couple of weeks after [[Noannar's Hold]], with the party's notoriety fading, a summons came from **[[Rennik Dale]]** — "the Gatekeeper," a general merchant and the best fixer on the [[Waterfront]]. His good grace is a ticket upward.

They met in a back room at [[7 Sinners]], a street gambling den, and there met **[[Lorien]]** — human, twenty-four or so, a chronurgy wizard, dressed up for a business meeting and reluctant to say much.

The job: escort a person to safety. There is a bounty on her. It came out on the road that she is **[[Devon Dale]]**, Rennik's daughter, currently in [[Duskandale]].

She stole from **[[The Ashrit Circle]]** *(name unconfirmed)* — who present as a resistance movement against [[Caldren Voss]] but are in fact collecting money and support for other purposes. The bounty was picked up by **[[The Ravensworth]]**: two human siblings, legends among assassins, well above the party's weight, who are said never to miss. [[The Songbird]] and [[The Knife]].

## The road

Men posing as [[Caldren Voss|Waterbaron]] toll guards, demanding absurd sums. The party detoured around them. It does the Baron's name no good, and [[Lorien]] seemed pleased about that.

## Duskandale

A gem of a village, built by **[[Corvan Vallen]]** of [[House Vallen]]. Its name exists in Common and in Elvish, which struck the party as odd. Rich, peaceful, idyllic — a picture of a place.

A dwarf family welcomed them. They met [[Brek]], who keeps watch by lounging in an old windmill. In the evening the whole village gathered at [[The Hearthstone]] on the square.

There they found [[Devon Dale]] with her allies: [[Tak]], [[Sef]], [[Fren]], and [[Brisk]].

## She will not run

She would not be moved, and did not believe the siblings were coming. She produced **documents proving [[The Ashrit Circle]] and [[The Hand of Yartar]] are working together**, and said she was going out to tell the people. [[Vaelis]] made a genuinely moving speech and it did not work.

Then she performed — and it was magnificent.

> [!cael] Cael
> Hunted, honest, and beautifully told. He decided she was worth saving on the strength of the telling. That is exactly how he decides things, and it is exactly the thing about him that has gone wrong before.

## The mark

While she was outside, the siblings marked her: **a pin and a raven's feather.** She understood then that it was real.

[[Cael]] told her to shift her perception — the city, not the group; survival, not the cause. She broke, and accepted his plan: he would take her place while she ran invisible. She asked that [[Brisk]] come with her. Cael promised Brisk would follow.

On the way out he steered her eyes away from the window, and from [[Tak]]'s body hanging outside it.

## The run

Even running, she hesitated — ready to throw it all away and go back for [[Brisk]]. They got her clear and moving toward her destination.

Then [[Lorien]], [[Vaelis]] and Cael — still wearing her face — saw **[[The Knife]]** waiting for them.
