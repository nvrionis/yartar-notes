# Session 2 — Marked by the Unseen

## The parley

[[The Knife]] had them, and talked anyway. He knew things about the party. He said he does not kill without reason. He wanted only [[Devon Dale]].

And he had leverage: **antidotes.** The party had been poisoned that morning at [[The Last Pint]], where they had stayed the night before, given a well-received musical performance, and floated a business proposal as **[[The Harpies Lyras]]**.

[[Cael]], still wearing Devon's face, went forward to trade her for the cure.

**[[Vaelis]] revealed the disguise.**

The Knife handed over some antidotes in exchange for the direction she had gone, and he and [[The Songbird]] left to hunt her. Devon's allies took her files and left.

> [!cael] Cael
> The Knife now knows Cael works illusions. That is all he knows — a road-priest with a trick, which is what Cael has always claimed to be. It costs him nothing and he intends to keep it that way.

## The ambush

[[The Unseen]] hit them — a faction rooted in [[The Warrens]] of [[Yartar]], talked about the way people talk about the boogeyman.

It turned when **[[Vetel Kraye]]** arrived: a strange man, or not quite a man, carrying some private suffering and a real hatred of the Unseen. He helped them win.

The Unseen's caster, **[[Selda]]**, a high elf woman, was killed.

## Tom Farow

The one survivor. He gave them:

- Another ambush waits at [[The Last Pint]], under **[[Reon Sahtar]]** — a top man of the Unseen — with a larger force.
- **[[Elis]]**, a potion mistress in [[The Warrens]], as a possible antidote source. No guarantee.
- **How to pass as Unseen:** their rings, and the bandages they wrap themselves in. Wear both and you read as a member.

## Loot

See [[Party Loot Ledger]]. From the field: 3 heavy crossbows with bolts, 3 hand axes, 3 short swords, 36 gp, a grappling hook with 50 ft of rope, a sabotage kit. From [[Selda]]: 9 pp, a [[Selda's Signet Ring|signet ring]], a [[Selda's Spellbook|spellbook]], a scroll of *blight*, and a [[Sending Stone]].

## The cave

[[Vetel Kraye]] sheltered them. And he told them why he had been out there: **he was [[Devon Dale]]'s extraction.** A griffon, and a way out.

She never arrived. He does not know what happened to her, and neither does anyone else.

The only real answer to the poison, he said, is a mage to the east.

[[Cael]] worked on the party's condition and got the symptoms down. He could not touch the progression.

Vetel had his own road to walk. After a shared breakfast he said he would like to meet again if fate allowed — **the far side of the [[Ironspike Bridge]] in [[Yartar]]** — and they parted.
