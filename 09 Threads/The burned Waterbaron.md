# The burned Waterbaron

**What we know:** [[Caldren Voss]] took [[Yartar]] by coup. He executed his predecessor — a woman — and burned her. **Her handprint is still on the side of the bridge.**

**What we don't:** Her name. Nobody has told the party.

**Who would know:** Anyone in [[Yartar]] old enough. The registries in [[High Ward]].

**Cost of ignoring it:** A city with a martyr it does not name has people who remember her.
