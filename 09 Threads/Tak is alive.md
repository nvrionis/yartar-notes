# Tak is alive

**What we know:** In [[Session 1 - The Savage Daughter|S1]] a body hung outside the window at [[The Hearthstone]] and it was [[Tak]]'s; [[Cael]] steered [[Devon Dale]]'s eyes away from it. In [[Session 3 - Trail of Hope|S3]], on the road back for the horses, Cael saw Tak **alive**, talking to a gathered group.

**What we don't:** Whether the body was someone else, whether it was ever a body, whether he was taken and returned, or whether he was theirs from the start. And who he was talking to.

**Who would know:** Tak. Anyone still in [[Duskandale]].

**Cost of ignoring it:** If he was theirs from the start, everything the party did in that village was watched.
