# Duskandale is too perfect

**What we know:** Built by [[Corvan Vallen]] of [[House Vallen]]. Rich, peaceful, uniformly happy — the party's own word was *idyllic*. Its name exists in Common **and Elvish**, which struck them as odd at the time. [[Dell]]'s refugees were sent there.

**What we don't:** Why the Elvish name. Nothing else in the village explains it. Nobody followed it up.

**Who would know:** [[Corvan Vallen]]. [[House Vallen]], about which nothing is known because nobody has asked.

**Cost of ignoring it:** [[Tak]] was hung there, [[Devon Dale]] was marked there, and the village knew none of it. Now a hundred refugees are walking into it.
