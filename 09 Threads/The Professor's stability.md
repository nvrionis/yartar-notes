# The Professor's stability

**What we know:** He is working to close the rifts between the material realm and the [[Vanishing Tower]]. He also keeps sending people *into* it to prove that time is a collection of memories. He was [[Lorien]]'s mentor. He does not look stable. He is holding the party's antidote and is alone with it.

**What we don't:** Which of the two projects is the real one. His name. What he does when he finds out [[Beat]] opened his laboratory for strangers.

**Who would know:** [[Lorien]], who studied under him and has not volunteered much.

**Cost of ignoring it:** He is the only source of the cure, and the party is not in the room.

> [!danger] Brewing the only antidote the party has access to, unwatched, while they are in another realm.
