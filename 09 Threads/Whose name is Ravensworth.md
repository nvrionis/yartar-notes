# Whose name is Ravensworth

**What we know:** The siblings' real names are **[[Doman Vein]]** and **[[Iskra Vein]]**, learned in [[Session 3 - Trail of Hope|S3]]. So *Ravensworth* is not a family name.

**What we don't:** Whether it is a title, an epithet, a company, or a house they work for.

**Who would know:** The siblings. Anyone in assassin circles, where they are legends.

**Cost of ignoring it:** If Ravensworth is a house rather than a nickname, there are more of them.
