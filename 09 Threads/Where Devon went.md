# Where Devon went

**What we know:** [[Cael]] wore her face out of [[Duskandale]] so she could run invisible under her own. [[Vetel Kraye]] was her extraction — a griffon, and a way out. **She never arrived.** [[The Knife]] and [[The Songbird]] went after her with a direction the party gave them.

**What we don't:** Whether she is alive. Whether the trade bought anything at all.

**Who would know:** The [[The Ravensworth|Ravensworth]] siblings. [[Brisk]], [[Sef]] and [[Fren]], who left with the files and have not been seen since.

**Cost of ignoring it:** This is the job. [[Rennik Dale]] is owed an answer either way.
