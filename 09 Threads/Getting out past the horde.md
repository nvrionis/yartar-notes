# Getting out past the horde

**What we know:** Fifty to sixty [[The Orc Horde|orcs]], not all of them fighters, camped in [[Netheril|Netherese ruins]] around [[The Professor's Tower]]. Led by a towering female orc. The party went in invisible on a promise of safe passage to the door.

**What we don't:** Why a horde carrying non-combatants is besieging one wizard's tower. Sieges are not what raiders do.

**Who would know:** [[The Orc Warleader]], who has not been spoken to. [[The Professor]], who has not been asked.

**Cost of ignoring it:** The party comes back through the rift carrying [[Memory Shards]] and walks into the same sixty orcs with invisibility already spent.
