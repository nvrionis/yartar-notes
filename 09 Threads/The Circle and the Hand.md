# The Circle and the Hand

**What we know:** [[Devon Dale]] had documents proving [[The Ashrit Circle]] — a fake resistance movement against [[Caldren Voss]] — is in league with [[The Hand of Yartar]]. She read from them at [[The Hearthstone]]. **Her allies took the files and left.** The party never read them.

**What we don't:** What the Circle is actually gathering money and support for. What a poison-and-information guild wants with a fake resistance movement.

**Who would know:** [[Brisk]], [[Sef]] and [[Fren]], who have the documents. Anyone inside either organisation — the party has met nobody from the Circle face to face.

**Cost of ignoring it:** The Circle pays for assassins and is patient.
