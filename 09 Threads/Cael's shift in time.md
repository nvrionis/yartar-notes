# Cael's shift in time

**What we know:** While working the ropes of [[The Drawbridge]], inside [[Lorien]]'s chronurgy, [[Cael]] experienced a displacement in time. Brief, and wrong.

**What we don't:** Whether it was the magic, the place, or him. Nobody has followed it up.

**Who would know:** [[Lorien]], who has not been told. [[The Professor]], who holds that time is [[Time as Memory|made of memory]].

**Cost of ignoring it:** Unknown, which is the problem.
