## Rule

A name the party has *heard* is not a name the party has *confirmed*. Mark unconfirmed names in **bold with a note**, and do not quietly upgrade them. Half of what the party knows came from [[The Knife]], [[Tom Farow]] and [[Vetel Kraye]] — one assassin, one prisoner, and one stranger.
