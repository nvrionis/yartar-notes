# Devon Dale

Daughter of [[Rennik Dale]]. The job.

She stole from **[[The Ashrit Circle]]** and took documents proving the Circle and **[[The Hand of Yartar]]** are working together. The Circle put a bounty on her; [[The Ravensworth]] picked it up.

Found in [[Duskandale]] with [[Tak]], [[Sef]], [[Fren]] and [[Brisk]]. She refused to run — she did not believe the siblings were real — and instead told the whole story to the room at [[The Hearthstone]]. It was a superb piece of storytelling.

Marked outside that night: **a pin and a raven's feather.** After that she believed it.

[[Cael]] talked her into survival — think about the city, not the group — took her face, and sent her running invisible. She asked for [[Brisk]] to go with her; Cael promised Brisk would follow, and Brisk did not.

## Where she is

Unknown. [[Vetel Kraye]] was her extraction — a griffon out — and says **she never arrived.** [[The Knife]] and [[The Songbird]] went after her with a direction the party gave them.

> [!danger] Unresolved. This is the job, and nobody knows whether it succeeded.
