# Rennik Dale
*"The Gatekeeper"*

General merchant on the [[Waterfront]] and the best fixer in it. Standing in his good grace is described as a ticket to the next tier of work in [[Yartar]].

Father of **[[Devon Dale]]**. He put out the job to bring her home safe, and sent [[Lorien]] to hire it done rather than come himself.
