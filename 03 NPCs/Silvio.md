# Silvio
A gnome. Survived the massacre at [[Noannar's Hold]] long enough to reach [[Cael]] and [[Simon]] in the hollow beneath the dead.

He gave them his amulet and one request: **get it to his wife and three children in Silverymoon.**

Then he died in their hands.

See [[Silvio's Amulet]] and [[Promises & Debts]].
