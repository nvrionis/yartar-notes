# Vetel Kraye

A strange man, or something near one. He carries a private suffering and a real, personal hatred of [[The Unseen]].

Arrived mid-ambush in [[Session 2 - Marked by the Unseen|Session 2]] and turned the fight. Afterwards he sheltered the party in a small cave and fed them breakfast.

**He was [[Devon Dale]]'s extraction** — a griffon, and a way out. She never arrived.

He is the one who said the only real answer to the poison is a mage to the east, which is the road the party is now on.

## The standing invitation

He would like to meet again, if fate allows: **the far side of the [[Ironspike Bridge]] in [[Yartar]]**.

> [!question] What is he, and what is his own mission? He said he had one and did not say what.
