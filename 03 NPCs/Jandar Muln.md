# Jandar Muln
Caravan-master out of [[Yartar]], middling means, runs goods along the Evermoor Way and the Dessarin trade.

Hires [[Cael]] by the run. Trusts his blade and, more useful, his temper.

He is the reason Cael has a reason to be in the city — and to leave it for weeks at a time.
