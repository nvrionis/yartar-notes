# Dell
Guide and leader of the uprooted [[Evermoors]] village that was attacked by [[The Orc Horde]] in [[Session 3 - Trail of Hope|Session 3]].

After [[Cael]] pulled close to ten of his people back from death and [[Lorien]] handed over the horses and 3 pp, he promised the caravan would be **steadfast allies** to the party if they ever met again. Freely offered.

Lorien pointed them toward [[Duskandale]]. They may be there now.

See [[The Evermoor Caravan]].
