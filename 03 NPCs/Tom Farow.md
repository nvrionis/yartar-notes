# Tom Farow

The only survivor of the [[The Unseen|Unseen]] ambush in [[Session 2 - Marked by the Unseen|Session 2]]. Talked, freely, and everything he gave has held up so far.

He told the party:

- **[[Reon Sahtar]]**, a top man of the Unseen, was waiting at [[The Last Pint]] with a larger force.
- **[[Elis]]**, a potion mistress in [[The Warrens]], might be able to help with the poison. No promises.
- **To pass as Unseen:** wear their ring and their bandages, and you will be taken for one of them.

The party has the rings and the bandages. See [[Unseen Rings & Bandages]].
