# The Knife
**Real name: [[Doman Vein]]** *(learned S3)*

Male, human. One half of [[The Ravensworth]].

Held the party at the end of [[Session 1 - The Savage Daughter|Session 1]] and chose to talk. He knew things about them. He stated plainly that he does not kill without reason and wanted only [[Devon Dale]].

He offered antidotes for the poison taken at [[The Last Pint]] — and took, in exchange, the direction Devon had gone. Then he and [[The Songbird]] left to hunt her.

## What he knows about us

- Enough about the party to name it before the meeting.
- That one of them works **illusions** — [[Cael]] wore Devon's face at him and [[Vaelis]] revealed it.

> [!cael] Cael
> An illusionist priest is a man doing a trick for his supper. He is content for that to be the whole of it.

## Read

Not an enemy so far. He dealt straight, kept his side, and left. He is also, by reputation, above the party's weight.
