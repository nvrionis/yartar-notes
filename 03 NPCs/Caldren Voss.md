# Caldren Voss
Waterbaron of [[Yartar]].

Took the city by coup. He executed his predecessor — a woman, name unknown to the party — and burned her. **Her handprint is still visible on the side of the bridge.**

He proclaims himself a baron of the people, for the people. Many in the city are unhappy with him and his methods.

## Around him

- Men posing as his toll guards are shaking down travellers on the roads and doing his name no good. Somebody benefits from that. *([[Session 1 - The Savage Daughter|S1]])*
- [[The Ashrit Circle]] built a fake resistance movement against him and used it to gather money and support for something else entirely.
