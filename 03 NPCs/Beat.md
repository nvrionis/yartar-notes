# Beat
A construct in the service of [[The Professor]], and the one who opened the tower door.

He holds a distinctly personal worldview, which the party found both charming and useful — they talked him into fetching antitoxins from the master's **locked laboratory**, against a standing instruction not to disturb him.

> [!question] Beat broke an order for strangers on the strength of an argument. What else can he be argued into, and what does his master do when he finds out?
