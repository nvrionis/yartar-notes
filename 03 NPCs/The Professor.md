# The Professor
*Working name. He has not given the party his own.*

Master wizard of [[The Professor's Tower]], east of the [[Evermoors]]. **[[Lorien]]'s former mentor.**

## What he is doing

Closing rifts between the material realm and the **[[Vanishing Tower]]** of **[[Ilvaeris]]**.

He also holds that **time is a collection of memories**, and the expeditions into the Vanishing Tower are an experiment intended to prove it. Those two projects do not obviously agree with one another.

He does not look stable.

## The deal

He prepares the party's antidote. They go through the rift, close the far side, and bring back as many [[Memory Shards]] as they can carry.

> [!danger] He is brewing the only cure the party has access to, alone, while they are in another realm.

> [!cael] Cael
> Cael likes him more than is wise. A man who thinks the past is made of memory rather than stone is a man who has arrived at Cael's own suspicion by a different road.
