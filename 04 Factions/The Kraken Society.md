# The Kraken Society

Named by [[The KrakenWizard]] as he died beneath [[Noannar's Hold]], along with a promise that the party would regret interfering.

Their operation drowned a town: a sahuagin caster used the [[Blue Gem]] to raise a pillar of seawater inside the temple, and the water never stopped.

Nothing has come of the promise yet. That is not the same as nothing coming of it.
