# The Evermoor Caravan

Not a caravan — **an entire village of the [[Evermoors]]**, uprooted and moving for better ground, hit by [[The Orc Horde]] on the road east.

[[Cael]] saved close to ten of them from certain death. [[Lorien]] gave them the party's horses and 3 pp.

- **[[Dell]]** — their guide and leader. Promised steadfast alliance.
- **[[Torvin]]** — gave [[Torvin's Shield]] for his daughter's life.

They were pointed toward [[Duskandale]] and may be there now.

> [!cael] Cael
> A hundred-odd people who will tell this story for the rest of their lives, with his name in it. He is aware that is a kind of asset and would rather not think about it in those terms.
