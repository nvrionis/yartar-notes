# The Ashrit Circle
*(name unconfirmed)*

Presents itself as a resistance movement against [[Caldren Voss]]. It is not one. The movement is a front for gathering money and support toward some other purpose.

[[Devon Dale]] stole from them, took proof of their arrangement with [[The Hand of Yartar]], and they put a bounty on her that [[The Ravensworth]] collected.

> [!danger] They pay for assassins and they are patient. The party has not met a single member of the Circle face to face.
