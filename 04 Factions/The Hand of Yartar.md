# The Hand of Yartar

Criminal organisation gaining serious traction in [[Yartar]]. Their trade is **daggers, poison, and information**.

**Membership is women only.** They hire male goons, who are not members.

## Known

- Working with **[[The Ashrit Circle]]** — [[Devon Dale]] had documents proving it. Those documents left with her allies.
