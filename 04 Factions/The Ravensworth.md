Two human siblings, legends in assassin circles, described as above the party's power. Reputation: **they never miss.**

- **[[The Songbird]]** — [[Iskra Vein]] — female

They took the [[The Ashrit Circle|Circle]]'s bounty on [[Devon Dale]], marked her in [[Duskandale]] with a pin and a raven's feather, and are hunting her now.
