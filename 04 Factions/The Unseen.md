# The Unseen

A faction rooted mainly in [[The Warrens]] of [[Yartar]]. In the city they are talked about the way people talk about the boogeyman — closer to folklore than to a gang.

They ambushed the party in [[Session 2 - Marked by the Unseen|Session 2]] and lost, largely because [[Vetel Kraye]] hates them and arrived.

## Known

- Their caster was **[[Selda]]**, a high elf wizard, killed.
- **[[Reon Sahtar]]** is a top man, and was waiting at [[The Last Pint]] with a bigger force.
- **You can pass as one of them** with their ring and their bandages. The party has both — see [[Unseen Rings & Bandages]].
- [[Vetel Kraye]] has a personal grievance against them and has not explained it.

> [!question] A folklore gang in the slums fielding a high elf wizard with a signet ring. Somebody is paying for this.
