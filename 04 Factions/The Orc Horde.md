# The Orc Horde

Fifty to sixty strong, **not all of them fighting forces** — which means camp, families, or followers. Led by [[The Orc Warleader]], a towering female orc.

They attacked [[The Evermoor Caravan]] on the road, and are now camped in the [[Netheril|Netherese ruins]] surrounding [[The Professor's Tower]].

The party slipped past them invisible. They are still there.

> [!question] Why is a horde with non-combatants besieging one wizard's tower in a Netherese ruin? Sieges are not what raiders do.
