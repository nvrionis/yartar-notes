# Lorien

Human, apparent age 24. **Chronurgy** wizard.

Met at [[7 Sinners]], dressed up for a business meeting and visibly not used to it. He brought the party the job that became [[Session 1 - The Savage Daughter|Session 1]], and was reluctant to explain any of it.

## Known

- Apprenticed to **[[The Professor]]**, the wizard in the tower east of the [[Evermoors]].
- Restored [[The Drawbridge]] with his magic.
- Gave the party's horses and 3 pp to [[Dell]]'s refugees without being asked, and pointed them at [[Duskandale]].
- Took visible satisfaction in the fake toll-guards damaging [[Caldren Voss]]'s name.

> [!question] What is his stake in [[Devon Dale]]? He carried a job from [[Rennik Dale]] and would not say why it was him carrying it.
