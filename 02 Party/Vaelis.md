# Vaelis

Shadar-kai. Hexblade warlock.

Met in the caves beneath [[Noannar's Hold]], where he was part of the group sent for the [[Blue Gem]]. He is the one who destroyed [[The KrakenWizard]].

Pact of the Chain. He keeps a familiar, **[[Martis]]**, a sphinx of wonder.

## What he does

He gathers the last breath of those he kills. It eases their passing, and it gives him — briefly — their perception of the world at the end of it.

> [!cael] Cael
> This is the thing about Vaelis that Cael finds genuinely beautiful, and he has said so. A man who collects how the dying saw it. He is not sure Vaelis understands why it moves him.

## What Cael has noticed

He is close to death. Not ill in an obvious way — close to it, and detached about it, in a manner that is not ordinary.

## In play

- [[Session 1 - The Savage Daughter|S1]] — gave a genuinely moving speech to [[Devon Dale]]. It failed.
- [[Session 2 - Marked by the Unseen|S2]] — **revealed [[Cael]]'s disguise to [[The Knife]]** during the parley.

They work well together regardless.
