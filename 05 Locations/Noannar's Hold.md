# Noannar's Hold

Drowned. See [[Session 0.5 - Noannar's Hold Massacre|Session 0.5]].

A caravan stop with a temple of **Eldath** standing on a much older site — one that had belonged to **Leira** in ages past. [[Sol]] the sailor knew, and told [[Cael]].

The temple was the trap. A gathering was called, the doors were barricaded, a pillar of seawater rose inside from nothing, and sahuagin came out of it. The water did not stop. The Hold went under.

Beneath it: a cave system, the [[Blue Gem]], and [[The KrakenWizard]].

> [!cael] Cael
> A town praying to one goddess in a house built for another, and none of them any worse off for it. He has not stopped turning that over.
