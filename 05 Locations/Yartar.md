# Yartar

Home base, in the loose sense that the party keeps returning to it.

## Who holds it

**[[Caldren Voss]]**, Waterbaron by coup. He executed his predecessor and burned her; her handprint is still visible on the side of the bridge. He calls himself a baron of the people, for the people. Many disagree, loudly and quietly.
