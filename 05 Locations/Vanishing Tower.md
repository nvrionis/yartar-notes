# Vanishing Tower

The tower of **[[Ilvaeris]]**, god of time, memories and history. The party is inside it now.

A realm of its own: columns made of memory, and things living in it that feed on memory. Alien throughout, and dangerous throughout.

**The task:** close the rift on this side, and carry back as many [[Memory Shards]] as possible.

[[The Professor]] has been sending expeditions here as an experiment to prove that time is a collection of memories.
