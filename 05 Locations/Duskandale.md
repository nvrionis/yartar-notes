# Duskandale

A gem of a village, built by **[[Corvan Vallen]]** of [[House Vallen]].

Rich, peaceful, uniformly happy — the party's own words were *idyllic, like a picture*. A dwarf family welcomed them in. [[Brek]] keeps watch by lounging in an old windmill. Everyone gathers at [[The Hearthstone]] in the square in the evening.

It is also where [[Devon Dale]] was marked, where [[Tak]] was hung outside a window, and where [[Cael]] walked out wearing her face. **The village knew none of it.**

[[Dell]]'s refugees were sent here.

> [!question] Its name exists in Common **and Elvish**, which was noted as strange and never followed up. Nothing else in the village explains why.
