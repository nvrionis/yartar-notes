# The Professor's Tower

East of the [[Evermoors]]. Home of **[[The Professor]]** and his construct **[[Beat]]**.

**Surrounded** by [[The Orc Horde]], fifty to sixty strong, camped in [[Netheril|Netherese ruins]]. The party got in invisible on a promise of safe passage if they could reach the door.

Contains: a locked laboratory (raided for antitoxins, against orders), and **a rift** into the [[Vanishing Tower]].
