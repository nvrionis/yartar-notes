# Blue Gem

The object the second group had been sent into [[Noannar's Hold]] to retrieve.

A sahuagin caster used it to raise a pillar of seawater inside a sealed temple, from nothing. The water kept coming until the town was under it.

See [[Sahuagin and the Pillar]] and [[The Kraken Society]].
