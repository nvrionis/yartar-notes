# Coin of Tymora

Given to [[Cael]] by an old mentor from his road years, before [[Noannar's Hold]].

**A hole through the middle.** The tails side is **smooth** — and it did not used to be. Something was on it once.

It has magical properties. 
1. When Invoke duplicity is cast you can swap position with the duplicate
