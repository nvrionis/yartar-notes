# Memory Shards

Found inside the [[Vanishing Tower]]. The party's task is to bring back **as many as they can carry** — this is the price of the antidote.

They are [[The Professor]]'s data for [[Time as Memory]]. Things in the tower feed on memory, which suggests the shards are food as well as evidence.

> [!question] Whose memories are they?
